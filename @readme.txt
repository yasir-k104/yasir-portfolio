YASIR KHAN PORTFOLIO — QUICK START

1. Extract yasir_portfolio.zip.
2. Open the yasir_portfolio folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html.
4. Right-click index.html and choose "Open with Live Server" (if installed), or open index.html in your browser.
5. Before publishing:
   - Replace your.email@example.com with your real email in index.html (search with Ctrl+H).
   - Check the education semester line and edit if needed.
   - Add your real GitHub/LinkedIn links.
   - Build the two starter projects and update their status with real demos/screenshots.

IMPORTANT:
- The sign-up/login section is a front-end demo only. It does NOT create accounts, save passwords, or provide real authentication.
- The two AI projects are clearly marked as ideas/planned prototypes; do not present them as completed until you build them.
- Profile photo is in assets/yasir-profile.jpg.
